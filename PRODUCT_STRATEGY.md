## Risks and Mitigations Strategies
| Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|---------------------|
| Low-quality AI-generated content | Invest in training models with habits-tracker data; implement human review for templates |
| Limited user adoption | Focus on viral sharing features; implement referral program |
| Subscription resistance | Ensure clear value differentiation between free and paid tiers |
| Financial risk | Implement cost-effective development strategy and secure necessary funding |
| Competitive response | Implement competitive analysis and develop differentiation strategy |
| Customer acquisition | Implement customer acquisition strategy and develop customer retention plan |
| Technical issues during development | Implement comprehensive testing strategy and rollback plan |
| Market acceptance | Conduct market research and gather feedback from potential users |
| Regulatory compliance | Ensure compliance with all relevant laws and regulations |
| Data privacy concerns | Implement strong privacy policies; be transparent about data usage |
| Scaling challenges | Design cloud-native architecture from the start; implement caching strategies |
